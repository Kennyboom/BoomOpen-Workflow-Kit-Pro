# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **BoomOpen Workflow Kit** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy open-source engineering community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and technical rigor toward other contributors
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, engineering trade-offs, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive, evidence-backed code review feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community and codebase quality

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment, doxxing, or publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Deliberately introducing malicious prompts, obfuscated scripts, or credential-exfiltrating patterns into skills, hooks, or workflows
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, skills, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, documentation, and official project channels) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Reporting & Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the **BoomOpen Labs** maintainers:

- For conduct issues requiring confidentiality, open a minimal GitHub issue titled **"Conduct contact request"** with no sensitive personal details so a maintainer can establish a private channel, or use GitHub's built-in content reporting tools.
- For security vulnerabilities or malicious skill/hook reports, use private vulnerability reporting as documented in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) ([Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/Kennyboom/BoomOpen-Workflow-Kit-Pro/security/advisories/new)).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with full respect for the privacy and safety of the reporter.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers follow these graduated community impact guidelines:

1. **Correction** — Community Impact: Use of inappropriate language or unprofessional behavior. Consequence: A private, written warning clarifying the violation and expected repair.
2. **Warning** — Community Impact: A single serious incident or repeated minor violations. Consequence: A formal warning with restricted interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary Ban** — Community Impact: Sustained inappropriate behavior or harassment. Consequence: A temporary ban from all community interaction and repository contributions.
4. **Permanent Ban** — Community Impact: Demonstrating a pattern of violation, severe harassment, or malicious contributions. Consequence: A permanent ban from all project spaces.
