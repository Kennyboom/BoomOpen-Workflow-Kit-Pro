### 2. Smart-Hybrid Mode A / Mode B Gate, 4-Tier Elastic Swarm & 17-Language Routing

Rather than forcing heavyweight orchestration onto trivial edits or overloading one sub-agent on large features, `v5.5.0` routes every turn dynamically:

- **Mode A — Known-Target Micro-Direct (Tier 0)**: `<= 2` already-known files in `1` domain with `0` exploration needed and no `/cmd` execute directly under Pragmatic 2-Tier Coding Standards.
- **Mode B — Multi-Agent Orchestration (Tiers 1–3)**: Triggered automatically by explicit `/cmd` workflows, `> 2` files, `>= 2` domains, or **natural-language workflow intents across all 17 supported languages** (`en`, `vi`, `zh-CN`, `zh-TW`, `ja`, `ko`, `es`, `fr`, `de`, `pt-BR`, `ru`, `hi`, `ar`, `id`, `th`, `tr`, `it`).
- **Cognitive Load Sharding Law (`role#shard`)**: Each sub-agent shard owns **at most `3-4` heavy files or `1` deep research/audit vector** (`N_agents = min(maxParallelSubagents, max(ceil(Files / 3.5), Domains, Vectors))`, scaling from Tier 1 `2-3` agents -> Tier 2 `4-6` agents -> Tier 3 `7-10+` agents up to `maxParallelSubagents = 8` default / `12` max).
- **Direct Plan-to-Executor Dispatch & Scoped Warm Reuse**: Approved plans dispatch 5-Field Handoff Packets (`MISSION`, `SHARED_CONTRACT`, `OWNED_FILES`, `READ_ONLY_FILES`, `VERIFICATION`) directly to domain shards and reuse `role#shard -> conversationId` via `send_message` with `0s` cold-start overhead.

### 7. 10 Orchestration Laws (`L1-L10`) & Deterministic Self-Healing (`E0-E6`)

- **10 Orchestration Laws (`rules/CORE.md`)**: **(L1–L2)** Zero-Latency Entry & 100% Requirement/Ledger Integrity; **(L3–L4)** 4-Tier Elastic Swarm (`<= 3-4` files/shard) & Quality-First `Model: "inherit"` (never `flash_lite`); **(L5–L6)** 3-Wave Contract-First (`SHARED_CONTRACT` + disjoint `OWNED_FILES`) & Language Compliance; **(L7–L8)** Direct Plan Dispatch (`boomopen cook state|packets`) & Scoped Warm Reuse (`role#shard -> conversationId`); **(L9–L10)** 4-Layer Phase Ratchet & Clean Git Tree (Brain Artifacts first on Antigravity).
- **Error Classification (`E0-E6` in `rules/ERRORS.md`)**:
  - **`E0`**: Interrupted sub-agent -> Warm Auto-Revive via `send_message(Recipient: conversationId)`.
  - **`E1` / `E1b`**: Transient tool retry (`<= 3x`) / Deliverable cohesion guard (single cohesive Brain Artifact `<= 500` lines).
  - **`E2`**: Build, test, or `boomopen check --strict-phase --no-stubs` failure -> Targeted `File:Line` repair routed to owning warm `role#shard`.
  - **`E3` / `E4`**: Contract/requirement conflict (`autonomous_first` or `ask_question` UI) / Cross-shard contract drift rollback & delta broadcast.
  - **`E5`**: Deterministic CLI validator failure (`research`, `design`, `plan`, `cook`, `ledger`) -> Immediate warm auto-repair until exit `0`.
  - **`E6`**: Round-2 debug/review deadlock -> Cross-Engine Escalation via `boomopen conduct dispatch --engine cross_family`.
