## Quick Summary

This document set catalogs the **27 business features** (`BF-001` through `BF-027`) of **`@boomopen/workflow-kit` `v5.5.0`** (**BoomOpen Workflow Kit v5.5 — Multi-Platform Unified & Smart-Hybrid Edition**), classified using the MoSCoW prioritization framework. Features span the complete product surface — from source-repository global installation with SHA-256 ownership ledgers (`BF-001`) across 5 unified AI platforms (`BF-002`) through 4-Tier Dynamic Elastic Swarm sharding (`BF-008`), 3-Surface `/settings` control with `frontier-guardrails` `< 2.5 KB` Slim Guardrails (`BF-024`), deterministic CLI compilers, Wave 2.5 Spec Reconciliation, and CI gates (`BF-025`), Multi-Session Project Autopilot (`BF-026`), and Cross-Engine Multi-Model Councils (`BF-027`) — mapping directly to all 10 business goals (`BG-001` through `BG-010`).

## Known Gaps (`v5.5.0`)

| Gap ID | Description | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|-------------|--------|------------|
| GAP-F01 | Headline productivity claims (`70%` faster delivery, `70%` defect reduction, `85%` token savings) remain self-reported architectural benchmarks rather than third-party controlled studies | Behavioral KPIs in `BF-003` and `BF-007` are marked `SELF-REPORTED` alongside validated structural gates | Pair claims with deterministic `boomopen` CLI gates and `34` `node:test` suites; run controlled A/B benchmark tasks |
| GAP-F02 | External dynamic skill discovery (`BF-013`, `npx skills find`) relies on external network/registry reachability when local `compact-index.json` fitness falls below threshold | Niche skills outside the bundled `1,462`-skill vault cannot be fetched in air-gapped environments | Automatic offline fallback to `22` pre-loaded core + `1,440` local library skills (`1,462` total) |
| GAP-F03 | Cross-Engine Conductor (`BF-027`, `boomopen conduct`) requires locally installed and authenticated peer CLI engines (`claude`, `codex`, `gemini`) for live multi-family councils | Single-engine workstations cannot execute multi-family `Author != Critic` pipelines | Run `boomopen conduct probe` preflight and fall back cleanly to native `:team` Elastic Golden Diamond shards |
| GAP-F04 | Zero-telemetry privacy posture means aggregate adoption rates and `:team` debate convergence distributions are not centrally collected | Ecosystem adoption and round-convergence KPIs cannot be aggregated across external users automatically | Inspect project-local `.boomopen/project-ledger.json` and `.boomopen/conductor/runs/` artifacts per workspace |
